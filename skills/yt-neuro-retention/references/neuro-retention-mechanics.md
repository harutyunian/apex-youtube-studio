## 3. Empirical Study Summary (12 Mechanics)

| # | Mechanic | Core Study / Author | Key Takeaway |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **7s Click Lock & 30s Intro** | Retention Rabbit (2025); MrBeast Guide; Nir Eyal (*Hooked*) | Confirm click in `7s`; clear `65–70%` at `30s`; defer asks until after reward |
| 2 | **In Media Res Change** | Will Storr (*Science of Storytelling*); Prof. Sophie Scott (UCL) | Sensory cortex prioritizes sudden change over chronological exposition |
| 3 | **Cortisol → Oxytocin** | Paul Zak (Claremont) | Threat/conflict (`cortisol`) unlocks attention; human empathy (`oxytocin`) drives `82%` behavioral response |
| 4 | **Narrative Transportation** | Green & Brock (2000, 2002) | Mental imagery + factual coherence (`63%` vs `5%` recall); zero tolerance for fluff/errors |
| 5 | **Neural Coupling & Voice** | Stephens, Silbert & Hasson (2010 *PNAS*); QRDE (2026); Metricool (2026) | Expressive human voice drives higher social presence/trust than AI voices; audit raw auto-dubs |
| 6 | **Ovsiankina vs Zeigarnik** | 2025 Meta-Analysis (38 Zeigarnik + 21 Ovsiankina studies) | Resumption urge (`67%`, `74–100%` orig) holds only with visible milestones (`70% → 44%` without clear end) |
| 7 | **Reward Prediction Error & ABT** | Wolfram Schultz; Knutson et al. (2001); Nir Eyal | Replace linear *"and then"* with **ABT (`And–But–Therefore`)** + Tribe/Hunt/Self rewards |
| 8 | **Orienting Reflex & 45s Cycle** | Evgeny Sokolov (1963; 2002) | Habituation hits in `~45s`; shift visuals every `5–15s` (`J/L-cuts`), Pattern Interrupt `45–90s`, Re-hook at `3:00` |
| 9 | **Value-Charge Polarity** | Robert McKee (*Story*) | Every act flips charge (`-→+`, `+→-`); flat celebratory streaks drop `>=4%` |
| 10 | **Goal-Gradient & Endowed Progress** | Kivetz, Urminsky & Zheng (2006 *JMR*) | `4–6` chapters + visible progress bar + early `60–90s` quick win cut mid-video exits |
| 11 | **Cognitive Load Control** | Richard Mayer Multimedia Learning | Apply **Signaling**, **Contiguity**, **Coherence**, and avoid **Expertise Reversal** |
| 12 | **Peak-End & Session Bridge** | Kahneman & Fredrickson (1993); Zhao et al. (2019) | Climax + final `12–15s` bridge to next video drives Satisfaction & Session Time |
